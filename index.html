🎮 How it works

One boss per week. Each round, damage = Squeektoshi's real wheel work in that round × 10 × community buffs. 💤 Nap (0 work): the boss heals 250 HP — but 3 naps in a row charge Dream Energy, and the next running round hits for ×3. 🐾 Jog (1–499) and 🔥 Zoomies (500+) deal damage. Correct Oracle predictions earn +50 RP. Kill the boss before Sunday 23:59 UTC and every raider with 10+ rounds this week earns the Boss Kill bonus (+200 RP). No gambling, no token, no payments — naps score too, and the hamster is never forced or encouraged to run.
